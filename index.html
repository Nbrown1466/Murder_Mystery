THE SUMMONS · LA CONVOCATORIA

You are hereby summoned to an evening of dinner, secrets, and investigation among the members and guests of The Order of the Crow.

This is not simply a Halloween party. Once dinner is underway, the residence will become a physical murder-mystery and escape-room game. You will inhabit a character with private objectives, uncover physical evidence, solve puzzles, negotiate with other guests, and decide whom you can trust.

No es solo una fiesta de Halloween. Durante la noche, el piso se convertirá en un juego físico de misterio, investigación y escape room. Recibirás un personaje con objetivos privados, descubrirás pruebas, resolverás acertijos y decidirás en quién puedes confiar.
