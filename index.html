What to Expect

Come in costume

Wear any Halloween costume you want. Your costume determines your supernatural species when you arrive; your assigned character is separate.

Come ready to play

The game uses physical clues, locked boxes, secret correspondence, bargaining, investigation and social deduction. No acting experience is needed.

Protect the mystery

Your character may know things others do not. Keep private information private until you decide it benefits you to reveal it.
